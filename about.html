About FoodLink

FoodLink helps reduce food waste by connecting food businesses with organizations that can distribute surplus food to people in need.

Our Mission

To reduce food waste by connecting food businesses with organizations that can distribute surplus food to people in need.

Our Vision

A future where good food is shared instead of wasted.

Who We Work With

Donors

Restaurants, hotels, cafes, bakeries and catering businesses with surplus food.

Receivers

NGOs and charitable organizations distributing food to communities in need.

Volunteers

Everyday people who help transport donated food safely and on time.

FoodLink

Connecting surplus food with those who need it — one donation at a time.
