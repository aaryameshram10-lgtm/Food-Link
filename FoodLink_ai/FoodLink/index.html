How FoodLink Works

A simple three-step process that turns surplus food into support for the community.

1
Donors List Surplus Food

Restaurants, hotels, cafes and bakeries list surplus food that is safe and ready for pickup.

2
Receivers Request Available Food

NGOs and charitable organizations browse available donations and request what they need.

3
Volunteers Help Deliver It

Delivery volunteers pick up accepted donations and transport them safely to the receiver.
